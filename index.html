02.0 / WHY THIS MATTERS
“

A wrong answer from a casual chatbot is annoying.
A wrong answer about a drug interaction is dangerous.

General-purpose AI tools generate fluent, confident-sounding text — with no guarantee it's grounded in real data. In biomedical research, that gap between sounds right and is right costs time, money, and in the worst case, safety.

MANUAL CROSS-CHECKING

Researchers today manually cross-reference literature across multiple databases because no single tool is trusted enough to work from alone.
